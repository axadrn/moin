Short version: the moin website has no accounts, sets no cookies, shows no ads and never sells your data. The `moin` program sends us nothing. Your code, your apps and their data stay on your own servers and in your own bucket.

This policy covers the website moin.run. moin cloud, when it opens, gets its own section here before it stores anything.

## Who we are

The website is run by Berry Good Ideas LLC, 30 N Gould St Ste N, Sheridan, WY 82801, USA ("we"). We decide how the data described here is used. Write to [{{support_email}}](mailto:{{support_email}}) about anything in this policy.

## Website visits and server logs

To answer a request, the server must process your IP address, the time, the page you asked for, and your browser and operating system. The site logs the IP address, time, page and result of each request, without your browser, for security and troubleshooting. These logs are rotated and kept only for a short time.

Downloading moin from the site, such as with `curl -fsSL https://moin.run/cli.sh | sh`, is a website visit like any other.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure, working service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## Page views

We count page views with Umami on a server we run. Umami sets no cookies, uses your IP address only to tell the country and does not store it, and shows us only totals, such as which pages are read and which site sent a visitor. We do not use Google Analytics or any advertising network.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in knowing which pages help (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## Cookies and browser storage

The site sets no cookies, so there is no cookie banner. Your light or dark theme stays in your browser's local storage and is never sent to us.

## The moin program

`moin` runs on your laptop and your servers. It talks to your servers over SSH, and to the watchers you choose. It sends us nothing: no usage counts, no names, no addresses. Downloading or updating it is a download from the site or from GitHub, like any other. Your apps, their files, their databases, their env vars and their backups never pass through us.

A watcher you run yourself is yours. What it stores stays on its server.

If your apps hold personal data of other people, you are the controller for that data.

## When you write to us

When you email us, we keep your message and our answer, to help you and to keep track of what we said.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in good support (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## Who else processes data

We share data only with the services that run the site for us, and only what each one needs:

| Service | What it does | What it gets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hetzner Online (EU) | Hosts the site | The server logs, on our server |
| Cloudflare (USA) | Protects the site and carries its traffic | Every request to the site |
| Our email provider | Our email | The emails you send us |

Umami runs on a server we control, so no other company gets the page views.

We never sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising. We hand data to authorities only when the law forces us to.

## Data outside the EU

Cloudflare is based in the USA. Where it handles personal data from the EU, it is bound by the EU standard contractual clauses or certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

## How long we keep data

- **Server logs**: a short time, for security and troubleshooting.
- **Emails**: as long as we need them to help you, then deleted. Ask and we delete them sooner.

## Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, a copy of it in a common format, its correction or deletion, and you can restrict or object to its processing (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR). An email to [{{support_email}}](mailto:{{support_email}}) is enough; we answer within one month. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

## Security

Our server has no open port: traffic reaches it only through Cloudflare. No system is perfectly secure, though. If we learn that your data was exposed, we tell you without delay.

## Changes

When this policy changes, this page and its date change with it.
