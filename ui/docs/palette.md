Find anything, from anywhere.

Styles: `components/button.css`, `components/field.css`, `components/card.css`, `components/overlay.css`.
Needs `moin.js`: palette.

```example
palette
```

`moin-palette-group` names a group of options, starting where their text starts. It does nothing by itself and takes no selection.
