## Colors and long words

`moin-foreground` is the plain text color inside a quiet line. `moin-live`, `moin-running` and `moin-failed` are the colors of a state. `moin-break` breaks a long word instead of pushing the box wider.

```example
text-colors
```

## Big type

The sizes of a landing page. `moin-display` is its one promise. `moin-statement` is a section in a few words. `moin-lead` is the sentence after it. It stops at `--measure`, and `data-large` makes it larger from a tablet up. `moin-huge` is a sign-off word as big as the page.

```example
text-landing
```
