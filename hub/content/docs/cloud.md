Coming. moin cloud is two things we run for you, next to your server. Your apps stay on your server.

## A watcher

The same [watcher](/docs/watcher) you can run yourself, on our servers. Your server reports to it. When the reports stop, or a domain is down from outside, it alerts you.

## A copy your server can't delete

Your server holds the key to your own bucket. That key can delete copies, because old copies have to go. So someone who takes over your server can delete your backups too.

moin cloud keeps a second copy, next to your bucket:

- Your server can add copies. It can never delete one.
- Each copy stays for a fixed time, then goes.
- It does not replace your bucket. It is a second copy.

## What it can't do

moin cloud receives reports and copies. It sends nothing back that your server acts on.

- It can't deploy, restart or stop anything.
- It can't log in to your server. It has no key.
- If moin cloud is down, nothing changes on your server. Your apps run, your backups run.

## Your account

moin cloud is the one part of moin with an account. You sign in with a link we send to your mail. No password.
