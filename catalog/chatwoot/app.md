Instead of Intercom. A chat widget for your site, plus email, WhatsApp and social messages in one shared inbox. Conversations live in Postgres, backed up by moin. It needs its domain: its links point to it. Give it 4 GB of memory. Set the SMTP settings as env vars for invites and replies by email.
