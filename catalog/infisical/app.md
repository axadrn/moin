Instead of Doppler, or HashiCorp Vault for teams. Keep env vars and secrets per project and environment, with access rules, versions and an audit log, and pull them into your apps with the CLI. Secrets live encrypted in Postgres, backed up by moin. Keep the server's env vars safe too: they hold the key that opens them. It needs its domain: its links point to it.
