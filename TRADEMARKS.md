are not part of that license.

- use, change and run the code, also commercially, as the license allows;
- say that your product works with moin, or is built on moin;
- name a fork after what it does, and say it started from moin.

- call your fork, product or hosted service "moin", or a name that could be
  taken for it;

Asking first is always fine: open an issue.
